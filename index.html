About Us

A globally minded community, built for every major.

Global Business Club creates a collaborative space where students explore international markets, cross-cultural communication, and global career pathways. Through speaker events, workshops, and networking opportunities, we prepare CU Boulder students to succeed in global business environments.

GBC is open to all Leeds students — and students across CU Boulder — who are curious about global business, international careers, or operating in multicultural environments. No prior experience required.
